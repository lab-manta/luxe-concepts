Four steps, one team.

You deal with the same two people the whole way. Most renovations take 3 to 6 weeks on site.

  1. Consultation

    Nick or AJ comes to your home, listens to what you want and looks at the room. The design consultation is free.

  2. Design and selections

    We plan the layout and storage with you, then choose the tile, stone, brass and timber. You get a detailed quote and timeline before any work starts.

  3. Build

    The owners are on site at every stage. Waterproofing, tiling and joinery, with your floors and walls protected and regular progress updates.

  4. Handover

    Final touches, a full clean and a walk-through of your new bathroom. NSW statutory warranties apply.
