People search for this every month

We checked 47 searches a Sydney homeowner might type. Together they get about 4,700 searches a month. The biggest one, "bathroom renovations sydney", gets 1,900.

What a click costs you

A click is one person tapping your ad and landing on your page. It is a visit, not an enquiry. On average a click costs about A$20.79. The main search costs about A$21. The dearest one we found, "bathroom renovation quotes", costs about A$53.76.

One more thing. Almost nobody types "premium bathroom renovations sydney". Google shows no measurable searches for it. People type plain words and suburb names, so your pages and ads need those words.

    Treat them as a floor, not a fact. Semrush guesses spend from the ads it happens to see. It shows US$0 for Luxe in every one of the 12 months, including the months your ads ran. So it misses small local advertisers.

    What it takes to catch up

    Ads, SEO and your Google profile, worked on together. Ads bring enquiries now. SEO and the Google profile build the free traffic your rivals already get.

    How we worked it out

    A$100 a day × 365 days ÷ 12 months = A$3,042 a month.

    A$3,042 ÷ A$20.79 a click = 146 clicks, rounded down.

    The same sum gives the other two rows. It assumes the average click price holds. Real prices depend on your bids and how well Google rates your ads and page.

    For scale: these searches happen about 4,700 times a month. 146 clicks is small next to that.

    Your own records show A$35,712 of past ad spend brought 69 enquiry forms, about A$518 each. Those are your figures, not ours.

    These budgets are scenarios to talk through. They are not a quote or a contract.

    Ads

    Decide how many clicks a month you want, using the table above. Aim the ads at the words people type, such as "bathroom renovations sydney" and your suburbs.

    SEO

    Build a page for each area and service you want work in. Fix the sitemap, the phone speed, the photo descriptions and the business details. Earn links from other websites, month after month.

    Google profile

    Point the profile at your main site. Keep the reviews coming. Add photos and posts for each suburb you build in, so Maps can connect you to more of Sydney.

    The demand is there, and your reviews show the work is good. The gap is visibility, and every part of it on this page can be closed with steady work.
