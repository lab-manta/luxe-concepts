People search for this every month

What a click costs

A click is one person tapping your ad and landing on your page. It is a visit, not an enquiry. On average a click costs about A$30. The main search costs about A$31. The dearest one we found, "bathroom renovation quotes", costs about A$78.

These are Google's estimates, converted from US dollars at the RBA rate on 11 November 2026. Your own account will show your real prices.

    What it takes to catch up

    Ads, the website and your Google profile, worked on together. Ads can bring clicks straight away. Website and profile work takes longer and aims at the unpaid results where rivals show now.

    How we worked it out

    A$100 a day × 365 days ÷ 12 months = A$3,042 a month.

    A$3,042 ÷ about A$30 a click = 100 clicks, rounded down.

    The same sum gives the other two rows. It assumes the average click price holds. Real prices depend on your bids and how well Google rates your ads and page.

    For scale: about 3,620 searches a month are core full-renovation searches.

    What one job is worth

    Your median job is about A$62,000 and your average is about A$71,000 (your figures). A$200 a day is about A$6,083 a month. That is about 10% of one median job.

    We have not assumed how many clicks become enquiries or jobs. Your account data will show that.

    These budgets are scenarios to talk through. They are not a quote or a contract.

    Ads

    Decide how many clicks a month you want, using the table above. Aim the ads at the words people type, such as "bathroom renovations sydney" and your suburbs.

    Website

    Build a page for each area and service you want work in. Fix the sitemap, the phone speed, the photo descriptions and the business details. Earn links from other websites over time.

    Google profile

    Point the profile at your main site. Keep the reviews coming. Add photos and posts for each suburb you build in.
