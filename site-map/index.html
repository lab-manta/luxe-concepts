06

Page layouts

Sixteen layouts, top to bottom. Every page of a type shares one layout, so the site stays consistent. Dark blocks are the first screen, gold blocks are the consultation form. Hover a block to read it.

Home (refined landing page)

  1. Header on the dark hero: logo, main menu, phone, Book a consultation
  2. Hero: headline and sub-line as on the refined page, offer line with its T&C line, Google rating, licence line, Book and call buttons
  3. Two owners: Nick and AJ, on site on every job, link to About
  4. Trust strip: Google rating, NSW licence 460103C, owners on every job, NSW statutory warranties
  5. Our work: three to four project cards, link to Our Work
  6. Why we're different: the refined page's comparison block
  7. Everything handled: what every renovation includes, with underfloor heating and the T&C line
  8. Reviews: three full reviews, still, linked to Google
  9. Before you ask: four questions expanded, each linking to the page or post that owns the full answer
  10. From the blog: three latest posts (title, one line, link)
  11. Consult form block (#consult)
  12. Footer; mobile sticky Book and Call bar

Service page

  1. Breadcrumb
  2. First screen: real photo, H1 with the service and Sydney, opening sentence that starts with the target phrase, offer line and T&C line (bathroom only), rating, licence, Book and call
  3. What the renovation includes
  4. Rooms and home types we work on (apartments and strata, terraces, family homes)
  5. Price guide (only figures the business approves)
  6. Timeline (one line, link to the timeline post)
  7. Recent projects: three project cards (kitchen projects added as they are photographed)
  8. Areas we serve: region hub links
  9. Planning guides: up to four blog posts that support this service
  10. FAQ block, expanded
  11. Consult form block

Guide page (cost guide)

  1. Breadcrumb
  2. H1 and a short answer first (starting price and bands once approved)
  3. Published and updated dates, author line
  4. Cost drivers as an HTML table
  5. What is included in the price
  6. Room by room: ensuite, small bathroom, main bathroom
  7. Honest answers to low-budget questions
  8. Related posts
  9. FAQ block
  10. Consult form block

Offer landing page

  1. Lighter header: logo, phone, Book
  2. First screen: H1 with the offer, the T&C line in readable 14 px type, real photo of an installed floor, Book and call
  3. What you get (type, thermostat, heated area, once confirmed)
  4. Why it matters in a bathroom
  5. Who it applies to: every bathroom renovation we design and build
  6. Terms summary and link to the full offer terms
  7. Two reviews
  8. Link to the running-cost post
  9. Consult form block

Our Work gallery

  1. H1 and one or two lines
  2. Filters: Room, Region and Suburb, on one URL (filter states are not indexed)
  3. Project grid: photo, project name, suburb, room, two finish words
  4. How a project runs, link to Our Process
  5. Consult form block

Project page

  1. Breadcrumb
  2. Gallery (portrait and landscape kept as shot)
  3. The brief
  4. What we changed (layout, plumbing, waterproofing)
  5. Finishes and fixtures
  6. Underfloor heating, if installed
  7. Timeline
  8. Client quote with consent

  10. Consult form block

Company page (About, Our Process, Testimonials)

  1. Breadcrumb
  2. H1 and opening
  3. Page body (owners and licence details; process steps; or reviews in full)
  4. Owner profiles with anchors #nick and #aj (About only): photo, role, years in the trade, licence role, posts written
  5. Reviews strip
  6. Consult form block

  5. Body: H2 sections in question form; comparisons as HTML tables
  6. From our jobs: one section of first-hand detail from Luxe projects
  7. One in-text link to the money page the post supports, near the top, with a descriptive anchor
  8. Related project card
  9. FAQ block (questions from Google's People Also Ask), expanded
  10. Author box
  11. Offer band with T&C line (bathroom posts only)
  12. Consult form block
  13. Related posts (two or three from the same topic)
  14. Sources: official pages cited, with the date checked

How pages link to each other

Linking rules that help visitors move on and help Google understand the site.

  • Home→Service pages

    Home links to Bathroom (first) and Kitchen with descriptive anchors.

  • Home→Blog posts

    Home shows the three latest posts.

  • Service pages→Region pages

    Bathroom links to every region hub; hubs link back to Bathroom with a varied anchor.

  • Service pages→Blog posts

    Each service page links to three or four posts in its Planning guides block.

  • Service pages→Projects

    Each service page shows three project cards.

  • Region pages→Suburb pages

    Each hub links to every suburb page in its region.

  • Suburb pages→Region pages

    Each suburb page links up to its hub (breadcrumb and body).

  • Suburb pages→Service pages

    Each suburb page links to Bathroom with a varied anchor, never the bare phrase 'bathroom renovations sydney' every time, and has one kitchen line linking to Kitchen.

  • Suburb pages→Suburb pages

    Each suburb page links to two or three nearby suburb pages.

  • Suburb pages→Projects

    A suburb page links to its local job, or names the nearest published job and says so.

  • Suburb pages→Blog posts

    The strata notes on Bondi, Coogee, North Sydney, Cronulla and Randwick (68% or more flats, ABS 2021) link to the strata post; Leichhardt, Balmain and Bondi link to the layout post; Bondi, Balmain, Leichhardt and Bellevue Hill link to the council post from their council note.

  • Projects→Suburb pages

    Each project page links to its suburb page with the anchor 'bathroom renovations in <Suburb>'.

  • Projects→Service pages

    Each project page links to its service page.

  • Blog posts→Service pages

    Every post links to its main money page (the first page in its Supports list) in the first third of the text, with a descriptive anchor that is not the exact target phrase of a suburb page.

  • Blog posts→Suburb pages

    Posts link to suburb pages only where the topic fits the homes there (strata, layout, council, small bathrooms).

  • Blog posts→Offer page

    Heating and small-bathroom posts link to the offer page; every bathroom post carries the offer band with its T&C line.

  • Blog posts→Blog posts

    Each post links to two or three posts on the same topic; never a long list.

  • Blog posts→Projects

    A post shows a related project card where a real job fits the topic.

  • Blog index→Blog posts

    The blog index links to every post; it is linked from the main menu, the footer Resources column and Home.

  • Company pages→Blog posts

    FAQ answers link to the post that owns the full answer; About links to each owner's posts.

  • Offer page→Blog posts

    The offer page links to the running-cost post.

  • Every page→Consult form

    Every page has a Book button to #consult or /contact-us/.

  • Every page→Every page

    Plain links in the page HTML, no nofollow on internal links, anchors describe the page they point to, and every page is linked from at least one other page.
